🔍 Week 5–6 — Marketing Attribution Analysis

Objective

Understand how different marketing channels contribute to conversions.

Customer journeys were created using multiple marketing touchpoints.

Example Customer Journey

Instagram
    ↓
Google Ads
    ↓
Email
    ↓
Purchase

Attribution Models Used

1. First-Touch Attribution

100% of the conversion value is assigned to the first marketing touchpoint.

Instagram → Google Ads → Email → Purchase

Instagram = 100%
Google Ads = 0%
Email = 0%

2. Last-Touch Attribution

100% of the conversion value is assigned to the final touchpoint before conversion.

Instagram → Google Ads → Email → Purchase

Instagram = 0%
Google Ads = 0%
Email = 100%

3. Linear Attribution

Conversion value is distributed equally across all touchpoints.

Instagram → Google Ads → Email → Purchase

Instagram = 33.3%
Google Ads = 33.3%
Email = 33.3%

Analysis

The project compares:

First-touch revenue credit

Last-touch revenue credit

Linear revenue credit

This demonstrates why the same marketing channel can appear more or less valuable depending on the attribution model.

💡 Week 7–8 — Management Analysis & Recommendations

Objective

Convert marketing data into practical business recommendations.

The dashboard and attribution analysis were used to identify the three most important findings.

Key Finding 1 — Google Ads

Google Ads is one of the strongest direct revenue and conversion drivers.

Business Implication

Google Ads should remain an important performance channel while CPA and ROAS are continuously monitored.

Key Finding 2 — Email Marketing

Email generates strong revenue with relatively low marketing spend.

Business Implication

The company should invest more in:

Lead nurturing

Customer segmentation

Personalised emails

Automated follow-ups

Re-engagement campaigns

Key Finding 3 — Attribution Changes Channel Contribution

Different attribution models produce different views of channel performance.

For example:

First-touch highlights channels that introduce customers.

Last-touch highlights channels that close conversions.

Linear attribution recognises the complete customer journey.

Business Implication

Marketing budget decisions should not be based on only one attribution model.
